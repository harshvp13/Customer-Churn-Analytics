# Customer Churn Analysis for Telcom
Executive Summary

This project analyzes customer churn in the telecommunications industry to address revenue loss caused by customers canceling their subscriptions. The solution involved using Python to clean, explore, and analyze customer data to identify key churn drivers and high-risk segments. The analysis highlights patterns such as higher churn among month-to-month and low-tenure customers, which can significantly impact revenue. By acting on these insights, the business can reduce churn and improve customer lifetime value. Future steps include building predictive models and implementing targeted retention strategies.

Business Problem

Customer churn directly affects profitability, growth, and long-term sustainability. Companies care about churn because acquiring new customers is far more expensive than retaining existing ones. The purpose of this project is to understand which customers are most likely to leave and what factors influence their decision. By identifying churn drivers, the company can take proactive steps to improve retention and reduce financial losses.
